# Week 3 Reflection

## What storage service would you choose for FinTrust's compliance archive and why?

I would choose Amazon S3 Glacier Deep Archive because it offers the lowest-cost long-term storage for compliance records while meeting retention requirements for historical data that is rarely accessed.

## What was the hardest Python concept this week?

The most challenging concept was working with file paths and ensuring scripts could locate files correctly regardless of where they were executed. Using pathlib and script-relative paths helped solve this issue.

## What will you apply first on a real project?

I will apply structured logging and error handling because they improve reliability, make troubleshooting easier, and provide clear visibility into application behaviour in production environments.
